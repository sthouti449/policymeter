## Summary

## Cloud migration and cost discipline (Travelers)
At Travelers he works with the enterprise BI platforms Cognos, Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) and IPA (formerly TM1). He owned the backlog for the on-premises-to-cloud migration of nine applications across roughly 24 to 25 environments and nine lines of business. The migration finished three months ahead of schedule. He cites about $1.2 million in on-premises cost savings, and about $143,000 saved on a three-year MicroStrategy renewal through a total-cost-of-ownership analysis. ## How he works
He starts with the user and the decision the system must support, writes success criteria before code, builds the smallest version that can be measured, and reports results with an adopt, extend or stop recommendation. He keeps model costs visible from day one and uses the cheapest model that meets the quality bar.
